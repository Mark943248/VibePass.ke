General

What is VibePass?

VibePass is a platform that connects students with the best events, parties, concerts, workshops, and social gatherings. We make it easy to discover events, create events, buy tickets, and connect with your community.

Is VibePass free to use?

Yes! VibePass is completely free for all to browse events, create events, and register for free events. There is a small service fee for paid ticket purchases to cover payment processing and platform maintenance.

Who can use VibePass?

VibePass is designed for all who are interested in discovering and participating in events. Whether you're a student, event organizer, or just someone looking for fun activities, VibePass has something for everyone.

Events

How do I find events near me?

Simply visit our Events page where you can browse all upcoming events. You can filter by category, or use the search bar to find events based on your interests or location.

How do I register for a free event?

To create events, simply click the "Create Event" button on the organizers dashboard. You'll be asked to fill in the event details, and once submitted, your event will be published.

Tickets & Payments

How do I buy tickets?

To buy tickets, navigate to the event page and select the event you want to attend, select your ticket type and quantity, then proceed to checkout. You'll be prompted to enter your M-PESA phone number to complete the payment.

What payment methods are accepted?

Currently, VibePass accepts M-PESA payments for all ticket purchases. We're working on adding more payment options soon.

How do I get my tickets after purchase?

After a successful payment, you'll receive your tickets in two ways:

  • Your ticket will appear in your dashboard under "My Tickets"
Each ticket includes a unique QR code that will be scanned at the event entrance.

Can I get a refund for my ticket?

Refund policies are set by the event organizers. Check the event page for specific refund information. For issues, you can contact the organizer directly or reach out to our support team.

Organizers

How do I become an organizer?

To become an organizer, just sign up to get an account and that's it

How do I create an event?

As an organizer, you can create events by clicking the "Create Event" button in your dashboard. You'll need to provide event details like title, description, date, time, location, ticket pricing, and upload an event flyer.

How do I manage ticket sales?

You can track all ticket sales and revenue from your organizer dashboard. The dashboard provides real-time analytics including number of tickets sold, total revenue, and attendee information.

Account

How do I create an account?

Click the "Sign Up" button on the homepage. You'll need to provide your username, email address, and create a password.

How do I update my profile?

Log in to your account, go to your dashboard, and click on "My Profile". You can update your username, email address, and other personal information from there.
